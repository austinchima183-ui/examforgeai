## Understanding Your Child's Curriculum

### What Is the Nigerian Curriculum?

The Nigerian educational system follows a structured curriculum designed by the Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council (NERDC). This curriculum defines what students should learn at each educational level, from primary school through senior secondary school.

ExamForge AI is built around this curriculum, ensuring that all content your child accesses is aligned with what they are learning in school.

### Curriculum Bodies in Nigeria

| Body | Abbreviation | Role |
|------|-------------|------|
| Nigerian Educational Research and Development Council | NERDC | Designs the national curriculum for primary and secondary schools |
| West African Examinations Council | WAEC | Conducts the WASSCE (West African Senior School Certificate Examination) |
| National Examinations Council | NECO | Conducts the SSCE and BECE examinations |
| National Business and Technical Examinations Board | NABTEB | Conducts technical and vocational examinations |

### How ExamForge AI Aligns with the Curriculum

Every piece of content in ExamForge AI is:

- **Tagged with a curriculum body** (NERDC, WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB)
- **Linked to specific learning objectives** defined by the curriculum
- **Categorized by educational level** (Primary 1-6, JSS 1-3, SS 1-3)
- **Assigned a difficulty level** appropriate for the level
- **Rated for quality** by teachers and reviewers

This means when your child practices questions on ExamForge AI, they are studying exactly what they need to know for their school exams and national examinations.

### The 6-3-3-4 System

Nigeria operates the 6-3-3-4 education system:

| Phase | Duration | Levels | Ages |
|-------|----------|--------|------|
| Primary | 6 years | Primary 1-6 | 6-11 |
| Junior Secondary | 3 years | JSS 1-3 | 12-14 |
| Senior Secondary | 3 years | SS 1-3 | 15-17 |
| Tertiary | 4+ years | University, Polytechnic, College of Education | 18+ |

## Viewing Content by Educational Level

### Accessing the Parent Portal

As a parent, you have access to the **Parent Portal**, which provides:

1. **Child Profile**: View your child's educational level and subjects
2. **Curriculum Overview**: See what your child is learning
3. **Content Preview**: Browse sample content at your child's level
4. **Progress Reports**: Monitor academic performance and engagement
5. **Calendar**: View school events, exam dates, and assignment deadlines
6. **Messaging**: Communicate with your child's teachers

### Navigating the Parent Dashboard

When you log in, you see the **Parent Dashboard**:

- **Child Summary Card**: Your child's name, level, school, and current performance snapshot
- **Performance Overview**: Recent scores, practice activity, and subject performance
- **Attendance**: Attendance record for the current term
- **Notifications**: Important updates from the school
- **Quick Links**: Access to reports, calendar, and messaging

1. From the Parent Dashboard, click **Curriculum**
2. Select your child's **Educational Level** (automatically set based on their profile)
3. Browse subjects and topics to see what your child is learning
4. Click on any topic to view:
   - **Learning Objectives**: What your child should know
   - **Sample Questions**: Examples of the type of questions they encounter
   - **Curriculum References**: Links to the official curriculum standards

### Understanding Content Status

| Status | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|
| Published | Verified, high-quality content available to students |
| Draft | Content being created or edited (not yet available) |
| Review | Content being checked for quality (not yet available) |
| Archived | Older content no longer actively used |

Only **Published** content is shown to students, so you can be confident that the material your child accesses has been reviewed for accuracy and quality.

## Nigerian Curriculum Overview

### Early Childhood (Ages 2-6)

**Nursery and Kindergarten** focus on foundational skills:

| Area | What Children Learn |
|------|-------------------|
| Language | Basic English communication, letter recognition, simple reading |
| Numbers | Counting, number recognition, basic addition |
| Social Skills | Sharing, cooperation, following instructions |
| Motor Skills | Drawing, coloring, physical activities |
| Creativity | Songs, rhymes, creative play |

### Primary Education (Ages 6-11)

**Primary 1-6** builds core knowledge and skills:

#### Core Subjects (Primary)

| Subject | Description | Key Topics |
|---------|-------------|-----------|
| English Studies | Reading, writing, grammar, comprehension | Phonics, grammar rules, essay writing, literature |
| Mathematics | Number operations, geometry, measurement | Addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, fractions |
| Basic Science | Scientific concepts and inquiry | Living things, matter, energy, earth and sky |
| Basic Technology | Technology and ICT awareness | Computer basics, simple machines, tools |
| Civic Education | Citizenship and governance | Rights and responsibilities, national symbols, democracy |
| Social Studies | Society and environment | Family, community, culture, geography |
| Computer Studies | Digital literacy | Computer parts, typing, internet safety |

#### Elective Subjects (Primary)

| Subject | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Cultural & Creative Arts | Art, music, drama, crafts |
| Agricultural Science | Farming basics, plant growth |
| Home Economics | Cooking, sewing, hygiene |
| Physical & Health Education | Sports, fitness, health awareness |
| Security Education | Personal safety, community security |
| French | Basic French language |
| Nigerian Languages | Yoruba, Hausa, or Igbo |

### Junior Secondary Education (Ages 12-14)

**JSS 1-3** expands knowledge and prepares students for the BECE:

#### Core Subjects (JSS)

| Subject | Key Topics |
|---------|-----------|
| English Studies | Comprehension, essay writing, grammar, literature, oral English |
| Mathematics | Algebra, geometry, statistics, number bases, ratios |
| Basic Science | Biology, chemistry, physics fundamentals |
| Basic Technology | Technical drawing, woodwork, metalwork, electronics |
| Civic Education | National values, governance, human rights |
| Social Studies | Nigerian history, government, economy |
| Business Studies | Bookkeeping, office practice, commerce basics |
| Computer Studies | Programming basics, spreadsheets, databases |

#### Elective Subjects (JSS)

| Subject | Key Topics |
|---------|-----------|
| Agricultural Science | Crop production, animal husbandry |
| Home Economics | Food and nutrition, clothing, family living |
| Christian Religious Studies | Bible knowledge, Christian ethics |
| Islamic Religious Studies | Quran, Islamic teachings |
| French | French language and culture |
| Nigerian Languages | Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo literature and grammar |
| Cultural & Creative Arts | Visual arts, music, drama |
| Physical & Health Education | Sports, first aid, health education |

#### BECE (Junior WAEC)

At the end of JSS 3, students take the **Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE)**, which covers:

- Minimum of 13 subjects
- Students must pass at least 6 subjects including English and Mathematics
- The BECE determines placement into senior secondary school

### Senior Secondary Education (Ages 15-17)

**SS 1-3** provides specialized education in science, arts, or commercial tracks:

#### Core Subjects (SS - All Tracks)

| Subject | Key Topics |
|---------|-----------|
| English Language | Essay, comprehension, summary, oral English, lexis & structure |
| General Mathematics | Algebra, calculus, statistics, geometry, trigonometry |
| Civic Education | National values, government, citizenship |

#### Science Track Subjects

| Subject | Key Topics |
|---------|-----------|
| Physics | Mechanics, waves, electricity, nuclear physics |
| Chemistry | Organic, inorganic, physical chemistry, laboratory techniques |
| Biology | Cell biology, genetics, ecology, human anatomy |
| Further Mathematics | Advanced calculus, linear algebra, complex numbers |
| Technical Drawing | Engineering drawing, building plans, isometric projection |
| Agricultural Science | Agronomy, animal science, agricultural economics |

#### Arts Track Subjects

| Subject | Key Topics |
|---------|-----------|
| Literature in English | Drama, poetry, prose, literary criticism |
| Government | Political systems, Nigerian government, international relations |
| History | Nigerian history, African history, world history |
| Geography | Physical geography, human geography, map reading |

#### Commercial Track Subjects

| Subject | Key Topics |
|---------|-----------|
| Economics | Microeconomics, macroeconomics, Nigerian economy |
| Commerce | Trade, business organization, marketing |
| Accounting | Financial accounting, bookkeeping, company accounts |
| Marketing | Marketing principles, consumer behavior |
| Insurance | Risk management, insurance principles |

#### WAEC/NECO Examinations

At the end of SS 3, students take:

- **WASSCE** (West African Senior School Certificate Examination) by WAEC
- **SSCE** (Senior School Certificate Examination) by NECO
- Both exams cover 8-9 subjects
- English Language and Mathematics are compulsory
- Results determine university admission via JAMB

### Technical Education (Ages 15-18)

For students in technical/vocational schools:

| Area | Specializations |
|------|----------------|
| Engineering | Mechanical, electrical, civil engineering basics |
| ICT | Computer programming, networking, database management |
| Building | Bricklaying, plumbing, painting, carpentry |
| Automotive | Motor vehicle mechanics, auto electrical |
| Hospitality | Catering, hotel management |

### Tertiary Education

**Colleges of Education** (3 years) — Train teachers for primary and junior secondary levels.

**Universities** (4-6 years) — Degree programs in all academic disciplines:
- 100 Level: Foundation/general studies
- 200 Level: Core subject introduction
- 300 Level: Specialized courses
- 400 Level: Advanced courses + project/research
- 500-600 Level: Professional programs (Medicine, Law, Engineering)

## Supporting Learning at Home

### Creating a Study-Friendly Environment

1. **Dedicated Study Space**: Provide a quiet, well-lit area for studying
2. **Regular Schedule**: Help your child establish consistent study times
3. **Minimize Distractions**: Limit TV, social media, and noise during study time
4. **Provide Resources**: Ensure access to ExamForge AI and other study materials
5. **Show Interest**: Ask about what they are learning and celebrate progress

### Age-Appropriate Study Guidelines

| Level | Recommended Daily Study Time | Focus Areas |
|-------|----------------------------|-------------|
| Primary 1-3 | 30-45 minutes | Reading practice, basic math, homework |
| Primary 4-6 | 45-60 minutes | Subject revision, practice questions |
| JSS 1-2 | 60-90 minutes | Core subjects, homework, revision |
| JSS 3 | 90-120 minutes | BECE preparation, all subjects |
| SS 1 | 90-120 minutes | Building foundations, subject mastery |
| SS 2 | 2-3 hours | WAEC/NECO preparation, practice |
| SS 3 | 3-4 hours | Intensive exam preparation |

### Using ExamForge AI at Home

#### For Primary School Children

1. **Supervise their sessions**: Young children need guidance using the app
2. **Start with Beginner difficulty**: Build confidence with easier questions
3. **Focus on core subjects**: English and Mathematics first
4. **Use the AI Tutor together**: Ask questions and discuss the answers
5. **Celebrate achievements**: Praise progress and consistency

#### For Junior Secondary Students

1. **Encourage daily practice**: Even 15 minutes per subject helps
2. **Use the Study Planner**: Help them create a structured plan
3. **Review explanations together**: Discuss step-by-step solutions
4. **Focus on BECE subjects**: Ensure coverage of all exam subjects
5. **Monitor progress**: Check the Progress page weekly

#### For Senior Secondary Students

1. **Encourage independent study**: They should manage their own practice
2. **Focus on exam preparation**: Use past questions extensively
3. **Practice under timed conditions**: Use the timer feature
4. **Target weak areas**: Use difficulty and topic filters to focus
5. **Discuss career goals**: Connect their subject choices to future plans

### Common Challenges and Solutions

| Challenge | Solution |
|-----------|----------|
| "I don't understand this topic" | Use the AI Tutor to get a simpler explanation |
| "The questions are too hard" | Lower the difficulty level in Practice Mode |
| "I keep making the same mistakes" | Review the Common Mistakes section in explanations |
| "I don't know what to study" | Use the Study Planner to create a schedule |
| "I get distracted" | Set a timer for short focused study sessions (25 minutes) |
| "I'm anxious about exams" | Practice with past questions to build confidence |

### Past Exam Questions

ExamForge AI includes questions from past WAEC, NECO, and NABTEB examinations. These are marked with a **Past Question** badge and include the exam year and body.

Practicing past questions is one of the most effective ways to prepare for exams because:

- They show the format and style of real exam questions
- They reveal commonly tested topics
- They help students manage time under exam conditions
- They build familiarity and reduce exam anxiety

## Monitoring Progress

### Progress Dashboard

The **Progress** page shows your child's academic activity and performance:

#### Performance Indicators

| Indicator | What It Shows |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Overall Score** | Average accuracy across all subjects |
| **Subject Scores** | Performance breakdown by subject |
| **Topic Mastery** | Which topics are mastered vs. need work |
| **Practice Activity** | How many questions answered per day/week |
| **Study Streak** | Consecutive days of practice |
| **Difficulty Progression** | Whether difficulty levels are increasing over time |

#### Understanding the Mastery Levels

| Level | Description | Action Needed |
|-------|-------------|--------------|
| **Not Started** | No practice on this topic | Begin practicing |
| **Beginning** | Accuracy below 40% | Review explanations and re-practice |
| **Developing** | Accuracy 40-60% | Continue practice, focus on weak areas |
| **Proficient** | Accuracy 60-80% | Increase difficulty, try advanced questions |
| **Mastered** | Accuracy above 80% | Move to next topic, periodic review |

### Attendance Tracking

The **Attendance** page shows your child's school attendance:

- **Daily attendance record**: Present, absent, or late
- **Attendance rate**: Percentage of days attended
- **Calendar view**: Visual overview of the term's attendance
- **Notifications**: Alerts for unexplained absences

### Report Cards and Assessments

The **Reports** page provides:

- **Term reports**: Academic performance summary per term
- **Assessment results**: Individual test and exam scores
- **Subject rankings**: Performance relative to class averages (where available)
- **Teacher comments**: Feedback from your child's teachers
- **Recommendations**: Suggested areas for improvement

### Setting Goals

Help your child set achievable study goals:

1. Open the **Goals** section in the Parent Portal
2. Create goals like:
   - "Practice 20 Mathematics questions per day"
   - "Score above 70% on JSS 3 Science practice tests"
   - "Complete all SS 2 Physics topics by mid-term"
3. Track progress toward each goal
4. Celebrate when goals are achieved

| Notification Type | Description |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: Is ExamForge AI free?**
A: Your school provides access to ExamForge AI as part of their educational tools. Check with your school administrator for details about your subscription.

**Q: Can I see exactly what my child is studying?**
A: Yes! The Parent Portal shows you all the subjects, topics, and content your child has access to, along with their activity and performance.

**Q: Is the content safe for children?**
A: All content is reviewed by qualified teachers before being published. Content that does not meet quality standards is not shown to students.

### Technical

**Q: Can my child use ExamForge AI on a phone?**
A: Yes, ExamForge AI works on phones, tablets, and computers through the web browser or the mobile app.

**Q: Does it work without internet?**
A: Yes, your child can download content for offline use. Practice results are synced when they reconnect to the internet.

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.

### Privacy and Security

**Q: Is my child's data safe?**
A: ExamForge AI uses industry-standard encryption and security practices. Your child's data is stored securely and only accessible to authorized school staff and you as the parent.

**Q: Can other parents see my child's information?**
A: No. Each parent can only see information about their own children.

**Q: Who can see my child's practice results?**
A: Only your child, their teachers, school administrators, and you as the parent can see practice results. Other students and parents cannot see this information.
