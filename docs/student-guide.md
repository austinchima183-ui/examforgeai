## Using the Content Library

### Getting Started

The Content Library is where you find all the educational content available to you. You can access it from the main navigation menu.

### Browsing Content

The Content Library lets you browse content organized by:

- **Subject**: Mathematics, English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, etc.
- **Level**: Your current educational level (e.g., JSS 2, SS 1)
- **Topic**: Specific topics within a subject (e.g., "Quadratic Equations" in Mathematics)

### How to Browse

1. Open the **Content Library** from the main menu
2. Your educational level is pre-selected based on your profile
3. Choose a **Subject** from the list (e.g., Mathematics, English Studies)
4. Browse through the available topics and content
5. Click on any item to view it in detail

### Content Types Available to You

As a student, you can access all **Published** content, which includes:

| Content Type | What It Is | How It Helps You |
|-------------|-----------|-----------------|
| Questions | Practice problems to test your knowledge | Practice for exams and class tests |
| Explanations | Detailed explanations of concepts | Understand topics better |
| Worksheets | Collections of practice problems | Structured practice sessions |
| Reading Materials | Supplementary reading on topics | Deepen your understanding |
| Practical Guides | Instructions for experiments and activities | Prepare for practical exams |

### Using Filters

You can filter content to find exactly what you need:

- **Subject**: Pick one subject at a time
- **Topic**: Focus on a specific area you want to practice
- **Content Type**: Show only questions, explanations, etc.
- **Difficulty**: Choose questions at your level
- **Past Questions**: Show only questions from past WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB exams

### Searching

Use the search bar to find content quickly:

- Type keywords related to what you want to study
- Examples: "quadratic equations", "photosynthesis", "Nigerian history"
- Search works across titles and content text

## Practice Questions by Level and Subject

### Finding Practice Questions

1. Open **Practice Mode** from the main menu
2. Select your **Educational Level** (e.g., JSS 2)
3. Select a **Subject** (e.g., Mathematics)
4. Optionally select a **Topic** to focus on
5. Choose your **Difficulty Level** or keep it on "Adaptive"
6. Click **Start Practice**

### Practice Mode Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Adaptive Difficulty | Starts at your level and adjusts based on performance |
| Timer | Optional countdown timer for exam simulation |
| Instant Feedback | See if your answer is correct immediately |
| Explanation | View step-by-step explanation after answering |
| Progress Tracking | Track which topics you have mastered |

### How Practice Questions Work

When you start a practice session:

1. A question is displayed based on your selected filters
2. Read the question carefully
3. For **Objective questions**: Select the correct option (A, B, C, or D)
4. For **Theory questions**: Type your answer in the text box
5. For **Fill-in-the-blank questions**: Type the missing word(s)
6. For **True/False questions**: Select True or False
7. Click **Submit Answer**
8. You will see if your answer is correct
9. Click **View Explanation** to see the step-by-step solution
10. Click **Next Question** to continue

### Question Categories

| Category | Description | Tips |
|----------|-------------|------|
| Multiple Choice | Select one correct answer from options | Eliminate wrong options first |
| True/False | Determine if a statement is true or false | Look for absolute words like "always" or "never" |
| Fill in the Blank | Type the missing word(s) | Pay attention to grammar and context |
| Matching | Pair items from two lists | Start with the pairs you are most sure about |
| Ordering | Arrange items in the correct sequence | Look for chronological or logical clues |
| Theory/Essay | Write a detailed answer | Structure your answer: introduction, body, conclusion |

### Tracking Your Progress

Your practice history is saved automatically:

- **Questions Attempted**: Total number of questions you have answered
- **Correct Answers**: How many you got right
- **Accuracy Rate**: Your percentage of correct answers
- **Topics Covered**: Which topics you have practiced
- **Streak**: Consecutive days of practice

View your progress on the **Progress** page from the main menu.

### Study Planner

The Study Planner helps you create a structured study schedule:

1. Open **Study Planner** from the main menu
2. Set your **Exam Date** (e.g., WAEC May 2025)
3. Select the **Subjects** you want to study
4. The planner creates a schedule based on:
   - Time remaining until your exam
   - Topics that need the most attention
   - Your current performance in each topic
5. Follow the daily study plan
6. Mark topics as completed as you study them

## Understanding Difficulty Levels

### What Difficulty Levels Mean

Every question in ExamForge AI has a difficulty level:

| Level | What It Means | Example |
|-------|--------------|---------|
| **Beginner** | Basic recall and recognition | "What is 2 + 2?" |
| **Elementary** | Simple understanding and application | "Solve: 3x = 12" |
| **Intermediate** | Standard application of concepts | "Solve: 2x + 5 = 15" |
| **Advanced** | Complex, multi-step problems | "Solve: x² + 5x + 6 = 0 by factorization" |
| **Expert** | Challenging problems requiring deep understanding | "Prove that the roots of ax² + bx + c = 0 are real when b² - 4ac ≥ 0" |

### How Difficulty Relates to Your Level

| Your Level | Recommended Difficulty | Focus Areas |
|-----------|----------------------|-------------|
| Primary 1-3 | Beginner to Elementary | Basic literacy and numeracy |
| Primary 4-6 | Elementary to Intermediate | Foundational concepts |
| JSS 1-2 | Elementary to Intermediate | Core subjects |
| JSS 3 | Intermediate to Advanced | BECE preparation |
| SS 1 | Intermediate | Building strong foundations |
| SS 2 | Intermediate to Advanced | WAEC/NECO preparation begins |
| SS 3 | Advanced to Expert | WAEC/NECO/JAMB preparation |

### Adaptive Difficulty

When you use **Adaptive Mode**, the system adjusts question difficulty based on your performance:

- If you answer several questions correctly, the difficulty increases
- If you struggle with questions, the difficulty decreases
- The goal is to keep you in your optimal learning zone — challenged but not overwhelmed

### Bloom's Taxonomy: How Deep Is the Question?

Questions are also classified by **Bloom's Taxonomy**, which describes the type of thinking required:

| Level | What You Need to Do | Example |
|-------|--------------------|---------|
| **Remember** | Recall facts | "What is the formula for area of a circle?" |
| **Understand** | Explain in your own words | "Explain why the area formula works" |
| **Apply** | Use knowledge in new situations | "Calculate the area of a circular garden with radius 7m" |
| **Analyze** | Break down and examine | "Compare the area of a circle and a square with the same perimeter" |
| **Evaluate** | Make judgments | "Which shape gives more area for a fixed perimeter? Justify your answer." |
| **Create** | Produce something new | "Design a garden layout using circles and squares that maximizes planting area" |

## Reviewing Explanations

### Why Explanations Are Important

After answering a question, always review the explanation — even if you got it right! Explanations help you:

- **Understand the reasoning** behind the correct answer
- **Learn the method** for solving similar problems
- **Identify mistakes** in your thinking
- **Build confidence** by confirming your understanding

### How to Use Explanations

1. After submitting your answer, click **View Explanation**
2. Read through the step-by-step solution
3. Compare the explanation with your approach:
   - Did you use the same method?
   - Were there shorter or simpler approaches?
   - Did you make any unnecessary mistakes?
4. If you got the question wrong, understand where you went wrong
5. Try a similar question to reinforce the correct approach

### Types of Explanations

| Type | Description | When You'll See It |
|------|-------------|-------------------|
| Step-by-Step | Detailed solution walkthrough | Most questions |
| Conceptual | Explanation of the underlying concept | Theory and understanding questions |
| Visual | Diagrams and illustrations | Science and mathematics topics |
| Comparative | Comparing your answer with the correct one | When you answered incorrectly |
| Extended | Additional context and related topics | For deeper learning |

### Common Mistakes Feature

Many explanations include a **Common Mistakes** section:

- Highlights typical errors students make on this question
- Helps you avoid making the same mistakes
- Often includes the incorrect approach and why it fails

### Marking Schemes

For theory and essay questions, you can view the **Marking Scheme**:

- Shows how marks are allocated for each part of the answer
- Helps you understand what examiners are looking for
- Useful for improving your answer-writing technique

## Offline Access to Content

### How Offline Access Works

ExamForge AI supports offline access so you can study even without an internet connection:

1. When you have internet, content is automatically downloaded to your device
2. You can also manually download content for offline use
3. When you are offline, you can access all downloaded content
4. Your practice answers are saved locally and synced when you reconnect

### Downloading Content for Offline Use

1. Open **Offline Center** from the main menu
2. You will see content available for download
3. Select the subjects and topics you want to download
4. Click **Download** to save content to your device
5. A progress bar shows the download status
6. Downloaded content is marked with a download icon

### Managing Offline Storage

Your device has limited storage, so manage your downloads:

1. Open **Offline Center** → **Downloads**
2. See all downloaded content with file sizes
3. Remove content you no longer need:
   - Swipe left on any downloaded item
   - Or use **Remove All** to clear all offline content
4. The app will warn you if storage is running low

### Syncing When Back Online

When you reconnect to the internet:

1. The app automatically syncs your offline activity
2. Practice results are uploaded to your progress tracker
3. Draft answers you saved offline are processed
4. New content updates are downloaded
5. You will see a **Sync Complete** notification

### Offline Limitations

While offline, some features are not available:

- AI Tutor conversations (requires internet)
- Content creation and reviews (requires internet)
- Real-time collaboration features
- New content browsing beyond what you have downloaded

## AI Tutor Integration

### What Is the AI Tutor?

The AI Tutor is an intelligent study assistant that helps you learn more effectively. It can:

- Answer your questions about any topic
- Explain concepts in simple language
- Provide additional practice problems
- Help you understand difficult explanations
- Create personalized study plans
- Track your learning progress

### Accessing the AI Tutor

1. Open **AI Tutor** from the main menu
2. You will see a chat interface
3. Type your question or select a suggested topic
4. The AI Tutor responds with a helpful explanation

### How to Use the AI Tutor

#### Ask Questions

Type any question about your subjects:

- "Explain photosynthesis in simple terms"
- "How do I solve simultaneous equations?"
- "What are the causes of the Nigerian Civil War?"
- "What is the difference between mitosis and meiosis?"

#### Get Practice Problems

Ask the AI Tutor for practice:

- "Give me 5 practice questions on quadratic equations"
- "Test me on JSS 2 Biology topics"
- "Create a mock WAEC question on Organic Chemistry"

#### Understand Explanations

If you do not understand an explanation from the Content Library:

- Copy or reference the content item
- Ask: "I don't understand this explanation. Can you explain it differently?"
- The AI Tutor will break it down in simpler terms

#### Study for Exams

- "Help me prepare for my JSS 3 BECE Mathematics exam"
- "What topics should I focus on for WAEC Physics?"
- "Create a revision plan for my SSCE exams"

### AI Tutor Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Contextual Help** | The tutor knows your educational level and adjusts its language accordingly |
| **Nigerian Curriculum** | Responses are aligned with the Nigerian curriculum (NERDC, WAEC, NECO) |
| **Step-by-Step** | The tutor provides step-by-step explanations, not just answers |
| **Encouragement** | The tutor provides positive reinforcement and study tips |
| **Multilingual** | Supports questions in English, Yoruba, Hausa, and Igbo |

### Tips for Getting the Best from the AI Tutor

1. **Be specific**: "Explain osmosis for JSS 2 Biology" is better than "What is osmosis?"
2. **Ask follow-up questions**: If the first explanation is not clear, ask for clarification
3. **Request examples**: "Give me a real-life example of osmosis"
4. **Ask for practice**: "Now test me on what you just explained"
5. **Connect topics**: "How does osmosis relate to what we learned about cell structure?"

### AI Tutor Safety

- The AI Tutor is designed for educational purposes only
- It will not provide answers to active exam questions
- It encourages understanding over memorization
- If it provides incorrect information, please report it using the feedback button
- All conversations are private and not shared with other students

### Document Chat

You can also chat about specific documents:

1. Open **Document Chat** from the AI Tutor menu
2. Upload a PDF, image, or text document
3. Ask questions about the document content
4. The AI Tutor will analyze the document and provide answers

This is useful for:

- Understanding textbook passages
- Getting help with homework problems
- Reviewing past exam papers
- Studying from class notes
